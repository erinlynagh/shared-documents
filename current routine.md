### Schedule

AM:

cleanse → tone → vitamin c (if you aren't too irritated) → barrier serum → light moisturizer → sunscreen

PM:

- Retinal Night (Mon/Thu): balm → cleanse → hydrating serum → barrier serum --> light moisturizer → wait 10-20 minutes for skin to dry → retinal → heavy moisturizer
- Barrier Night (Tue/Wed/Fri/Sun): balm → cleanse → tone → hydrating serum → barrier serum → heavy moisturizer
- Exfoliation Night (Sat only): balm → cleanse → AHA OR BHA → hydrating serum → barrier serum → heavy moisturizer

As your skin starts to tolerate the retinal better over the course of a few weeks you can do it 3 or 4 times a week
